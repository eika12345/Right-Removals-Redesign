Terms & Conditions

Right Removals Terms and Conditions

By clicking “BOOK NOW”, you accept all of the following Terms and Conditions. All confirmed orders are bound by these Terms.

Vans Equipment

All our vans come equipped with trolleys, blankets, straps, bungee cords, tracking and sat-nav. Vans are used only for transporting furniture and household items.

We do NOT offer rubbish clearance, junk removals, or waste disposal.

Vans Sizes

We offer three van sizes. Make sure you choose the correct size for your move. You can view the full dimensions on our website.

Sometimes a larger van may be sent, but we will limit the usable loading space unless you choose to upgrade.

Quotation and Cost

We operate across London and nationwide. Your online quote includes all fees based on your selected order details.

The final price may change if:

  • Information provided by the customer differs from the actual job
  • The service takes longer than estimated
  • Additional loading or extra time is required

Price factors include:

  • Van size
  • Number of helpers
  • Distance (£1.50 per mile after 10 miles)
  • Floors without a lift (£5/hour per floor per person)
  • Congestion Charge Zone
  • ULEZ Zone

A booking includes one full load of the selected van. Additional loads are charged as one extra hour + added distance/time.

If you order one helper, the driver assists with loading/unloading. For heavy or large items, please book two helpers.

One passenger may travel in the van if there is an available seat, but not guaranteed. Always arrange backup transport.

Amendments

To change your quote (date, time, van size, helpers, addresses), please submit a new quote on our website.

To change an existing booking, use the Contact Form, email, SMS, or call with your order number.

Voicemail requests are not accepted.

Cancellation

To cancel, submit the cancellation through the Contact Form or by SMS with your order number.

Voicemail cancellations are not accepted.

Cancelling 48 hours+ before the move: full refund.

Cancelling within 48 hours: 30% cancellation fee.

Payments

Accepted payment methods:

  • Card (online only)
  • Cash
  • Bank transfer

A minimum 30% deposit is required to confirm a booking. The remaining balance must be paid before unloading.

Insurance

We provide:

  • Public & Employee Liability Insurance: £1,000,000
  • Goods in Transit Insurance: £10,000

Goods in Transit insurance applies only when the Inventory List is completed.

Damage must be reported before our team leaves. Once the team has departed, we cannot accept responsibility.

Packing

We can assist with packing/unpacking at the same hourly rate.

Do not overload boxes—keep boxes under 20–25kg.

Packing Materials

Packing materials are not included unless ordered separately.

You pay only for the materials used on the day.

Furniture Assembly / Disassembly

We can assist with assembling or disassembling furniture at no additional cost (standard hourly rate applies).

Please provide any specialised tools if required.

Parking

Customers must arrange parking and permits.

If no permit is provided, a refundable £65 + VAT deposit may be required to cover potential parking fines.

Included in the Price

  • Fuel charges
  • No charge for driver travel to/from your address
  • Working time from arrival to completion
  • Distance within 10 miles

Not Included in the Price

  • Packing materials
  • Parking fees, fines, tolls, ferry costs
  • Distance charges beyond 10 miles (£1.5/mile)
  • Congestion Charge
  • ULEZ fee

Delays

We aim to be punctual, but please allow a 1–2 hour window in case of traffic or delays outside our control.
